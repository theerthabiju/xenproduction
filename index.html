FAQ

Frequently Asked
Question

What services does XEN Productions offer?

We provide professional videography, video editing, commercial advertisement production, corporate videos, social media content creation, influencer marketing, AI-powered marketing solutions, photography, and creative training programs—all under one roof.

Do you create videos for businesses of all sizes?

Absolutely. Whether you're a startup, small business, established company, or personal brand, we create customized video content that aligns with your goals, audience, and budget.

Can you handle both filming and video editing?

Yes. Our team manages the complete production process—from concept development and scripting to filming, editing, color grading, motion graphics, sound design, and final delivery.

Do you offer influencer marketing services?

Yes. We connect brands with the right influencers to create authentic campaigns that increase brand awareness, engagement, and customer trust across multiple social media platforms.

What is included in your AI Marketing Solutions?

Our AI Marketing services include AI-generated content, marketing strategy, automated content workflows, AI-assisted video creation, social media optimization, and data-driven campaign planning to help brands grow faster.

Can you create content for Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook?

Definitely. We produce platform-optimized content including Instagram Reels, YouTube videos, YouTube Shorts, Facebook videos, TikTok content, and promotional advertisements designed to maximize engagement.

Do you provide videography and editing training?

Yes. XEN Productions offers practical training in videography, professional video editing, cinematic storytelling, AI content creation, color grading, and industry-standard editing software to help aspiring creators build successful careers.

How do I get started with XEN Productions?

Simply contact us through our website, phone, or email. We'll discuss your project, understand your requirements, prepare a customized proposal, and guide you through every stage of the production process.
